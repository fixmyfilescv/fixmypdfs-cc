FixMyPDFs

Fix your PDFs. Free. In your browser.

Powered by pdf.js, pdf-lib and qpdf compiled to WebAssembly, running inside your own browser. Free, with no ads, and it will stay that way — if it saved you a job, you can chip in.

Photos, video or audio to fix? Our sister site FixMyFiles does those the same way, in your browser.
