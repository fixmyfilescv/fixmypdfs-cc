Alternatives

Free alternatives to the big PDF sites

5 side-by-side comparisons. Each one names what the other service does better — a comparison that finds no fault with itself is an advert.

The big PDF sites are good at what they do, and several of them do things this one cannot. Acrobat has real OCR, accessibility tagging and PDF/A export. iLovePDF and Smallpdf have polished mobile apps, team accounts and integrations with cloud drives. If you need any of that, the comparison will say so rather than talking you out of it.

What changes here is where the work happens. Those services upload your document to a server, process it there and send back a result — which is why they can offer heavy processing, and also why there is a copy of your file on a machine you do not control, held for however long their retention policy says. Every tool on this site runs inside your own browser instead, so the document never leaves your device at all.

That trade has a clear shape. You lose server-grade processing and the features that genuinely need it. You gain no upload, no file size cap beyond your own memory, no daily limit, no account, no watermark, and tools that keep working on a plane. For a bank statement, a signed contract or a passport scan, most people find that trade easy.

Each comparison below lists their tools next to the equivalent here, so you can check whether the job you actually have is covered before switching anything.
