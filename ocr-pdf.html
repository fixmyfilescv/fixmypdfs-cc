    Why it doesn’t upload

    The PDFs that need OCR are usually scans of paper nobody wanted to retype: signed contracts, bank letters, medical records, old tax files. The text recognition runs in this browser tab after a one-time model download, so the pages are read here and never sent to an OCR service.

    Check it in your browser’s Network tab

    Worth knowing

    • Recognition is good on clean printed text and weaker on handwriting, faint copies, stamps and unusual fonts. Always check a figure that matters against the page.
    • Pages scanned sideways or upside down read poorly. Turn them the right way up first (Rotate PDF does that), then OCR.
    • Six Latin-script languages: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Indonesian. Other scripts — Hindi, Arabic, Chinese, Cyrillic — aren’t supported yet.
    • The page picture is kept as it is; this adds a text layer, it doesn’t clean up or shrink the scan. The file grows a little.
    • It runs on your device, so a 100-page scan takes several minutes, faster on a laptop than a phone. Keep the tab open until it finishes.

    Under the hood

    What happens to your file

    Each page is drawn by pdf.js at 300 dpi and read by Tesseract’s LSTM engine, compiled to WebAssembly and run in a Web Worker, with the language model served from this site. Every word is then written back as invisible text (render mode 3) on its baseline, stretched to the word’s exact width, so search and selection line up with what you see. The page picture itself is not changed.

    Questions

    How do I make a scanned PDF searchable for free?

    Drop it here, pick the language and press Make searchable. Every page is read in your browser and an invisible text layer is added under the words, so Ctrl+F, selecting and copying work — with no upload, account or watermark.

    Is it safe to OCR a confidential document here?

    Yes. The OCR engine and the language model download to your browser once, and the pages are read inside this tab. Nothing is sent anywhere — you can switch off Wi-Fi after the model loads and it still works.

    Will the PDF look different after OCR?

    No. The page picture stays exactly as it was; the recognized text sits invisibly underneath it, lined up with each word. It only shows when you search or select.

    Why can’t I search my scanned PDF?

    A scan is a picture of words, with no text in it for a search to find. OCR reads the picture and adds that text. Once this tool has run, search works in every viewer, from Chrome to Acrobat.

    How accurate is the OCR?

    On a clean, straight scan of printed text, nearly every word is right. Each result shows an average confidence score; low scores mean a faint, skewed or handwritten page, where you should check the text before relying on it.
