    Why it doesn’t upload

    The PDFs people turn into images are often certificates, tickets, statements and IDs. Rendering them locally means the document is never copied to a stranger’s server.

    Check it in your browser’s Network tab

    Worth knowing

    • Password-protected PDFs need their password removed first.
    • Very large pages at 600 DPI (posters, drawings) are scaled to what your device can hold in memory.
    • Transparent areas are rendered on white.
    • To get the photos inside a PDF at their original quality instead of whole pages, extract the images.

    Under the hood

    What happens to your file

    pdf.js renders each page at the resolution you choose, the same renderer Firefox uses to show PDFs. Images are encoded in a worker, with the browser’s encoder or MozJPEG and WebP codecs compiled to WebAssembly, and several pages come back as one ZIP.

    Questions

    How do I convert a PDF to JPG for free?

    Drop the PDF here, choose JPG and a resolution, and press Convert pages. Every page becomes a JPG — no watermark, no sign-up.

    What DPI should I use?

    150 DPI looks sharp on any screen and keeps files small. 300 DPI is print quality. 600 DPI is for archiving, zooming in or printing large.

    JPG or PNG — which is better for PDF pages?

    PNG keeps text perfectly crisp and is best for documents with little color. JPG makes much smaller files for pages full of photos.

    Can I convert just one page?

    Yes. Type the page number in Pages — or several, like 1, 3-5.

    How do I get the pictures out of a PDF instead?

    Extract Images from PDF saves each embedded photo on its own, at its original quality, without the rest of the page.
