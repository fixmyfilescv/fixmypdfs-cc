Who it’s for

PDF tools, arranged around your job

8 professions, each with a workflow built from tools that work today.

Every tool on this site is on the tools page, and for most people that list is the wrong shape. Someone does not arrive wanting "a split tool" — they arrive with a month of batched invoices that need to become one file per invoice, or a disclosure bundle that has to be redacted before Friday.

These pages are the same tools in that order instead. Each one names the problems that profession actually hits, then walks a route through four to six tools that work today, saying what each does for that reader specifically.

Each page is also explicit about what this site cannot do for that job — certified e-signatures, Bates numbering, OCR — because finding that out four steps in is worse than being told at the start.

Professions

8
  • PDF tools for lawyers Redaction that removes text for good, SHA-256 verification for exhibits and metadata stripping — in your browser, so privileged files never leave your machine.
  • PDF tools for accountants Turn bank statements into spreadsheets, split batched invoices into separate files and combine tax documents — in your browser, with nothing ever uploaded.
  • PDF tools for students Merge lecture notes, shrink a dissertation under a submission limit, extract the chapter you need and turn slides into handouts. Free, unlimited, no account.
  • PDF tools for teachers Build worksheet packs, split a textbook into the pages you need, make printable handouts and share materials that open on every device. Free and unlimited.
  • PDF tools for HR Anonymize CVs for fair shortlisting, assemble employee files, password-protect contracts and clear metadata — with personal data never leaving your machine.
  • PDF tools for freelancers Send invoices that keep their layout, combine receipts for a tax return, protect a proposal and fit files under a client portal limit. Free, no subscription.
  • PDF tools for real estate agents Combine listing packs, shrink brochures to email, redact personal details from offers and rotate scanned documents. Free, unlimited, nothing uploaded.
  • PDF tools for small business Invoices, quotes, signed contracts and supplier records — assembled, compressed and protected in your browser. No subscription, no per-seat cost, no upload.
