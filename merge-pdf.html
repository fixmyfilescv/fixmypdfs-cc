    Why it doesn’t upload

    Merging usually means putting your most personal documents in one place — IDs, payslips, certificates, statements. Doing it in your browser means that combined file never exists on anyone else’s server.

    Check it in your browser’s Network tab

    Worth knowing

    • Original bookmarks aren’t carried across; a new bookmark is added for each file instead.
    • Fillable form fields may stop being editable after merging, so fill forms in first.
    • Password-protected PDFs need their password removed first. Files that only restrict printing merge fine.
    • Very large merges are held in memory; on a phone, keep the total under a few hundred MB.

    Under the hood

    What happens to your file

    Each page object is copied from its source file into a new document with pdf-lib, in the order you set — the same page, not a re-rendered picture of it, so text stays selectable and scans keep their exact quality. Images you add are placed on pages of their own. Only fonts and images the kept pages use are carried over.

    Runs on pdf-lib

    Reference: pdf-lib documentation · ISO 32000-2 (PDF 2.0)

    Questions

    How do I merge PDF files for free?

    Drop them here, use the arrows to set the order and press Merge files. There’s no watermark, no sign-up and no limit on how many files you combine.

    How do I combine PDF files in a specific order?

    After adding the files, move each one up or down with its arrows. The merged PDF follows the list from top to bottom. To reorder individual pages instead, use Organize Pages.

    How do I merge PDF documents for free without Adobe?

    Drop the documents here — contracts, statements, scans, anything saved as a PDF — set the order and press Merge files. There is nothing to install, no Acrobat subscription and no account; it works in any browser.

    Can I merge photos and PDFs together?

    Yes. JPG, PNG, WebP and iPhone HEIC photos each become their own page, wherever you place them in the list.

    Does merging reduce the quality?

    No. Pages are copied exactly — text stays text and images keep their resolution. Only if you switch on compression for a size limit are images inside recompressed.

    Can I merge PDFs without uploading them?

    That’s how this works: the files are combined inside your browser tab. You can even switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads.
